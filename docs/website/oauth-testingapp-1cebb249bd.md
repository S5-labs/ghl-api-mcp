# Step 4: Installing and Testing a Marketplace App

This section explains how to generate a **Test Link** for a specific app version in the **Developer Marketplace** and use it to **install the app into a specific location/agency account** for testing.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

- Access to the **Sandbox HighLevel account**
- Access to the **Developer Marketplace Account**

---

## Steps

1. **Log in** to the **Sandbox account** and the **Developer Marketplace Account**.
1. Go to **My Apps** and click the **app** you want to test.
1. From the left-hand navigation, click **Manage**.
1. Click **Versions**.
1. Find the version you want to test and click the **three dots (⋯)** menu.
1. Click **Test Link** and provide the **Location ID** of the account where you want to install and test the app.
1. **Copy** the install link that is generated.
1. Open a **new browser tab** and paste the install link.
1. Click the **Install** button you see in the app details section.
1. Complete the installation and proceed with testing the app.

---

## Output / Expected Result

- The app version is installed into the **Location ID** you provided.
- You can now proceed with functional testing (OAuth, API calls, webhooks, custom workflow actions & triggers, custom page, etc.).
